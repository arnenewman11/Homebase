Key idea

Five layers, built one at a time

Each layer works on its own. Layer 1 is useful by itself even if nothing else ever gets built.

LAYER 1

Snapshot

Every account and debt, grouped into buckets. Updated monthly.

LAYER 2

Runway model

Month-by-month: money in, money out, which bucket covers the gap.

LAYER 3

Guardrails

Four numbers with green, yellow and red zones you choose.

LAYER 4

Time scoring

Rates each income and hobby track. Shifts toward cash when guardrails turn yellow.

LAYER 5

Weekly tasks

A short list pushed to Todoist after a 20-minute weekly review.

The most important idea: not all money costs the same to spend. Cash costs nothing to use. Taxable investments may cost some tax. Traditional retirement money before age 59½ usually costs income tax plus a 10% penalty, and loses decades of growth. The model's main job is to always pull from the cheapest bucket first, and to warn you early, before an expensive withdrawal becomes the only option.
