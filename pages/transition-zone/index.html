Class outcomes

  • Move through the zone confidently, in short controlled advances
  • Know where to stand and why, moving as a pair
  • Hit soft shots while working forward
  • Reset and neutralize hard balls
  • Make better decisions under pressure
  • Spot when to attack instead of just survive

Rules of the zone

#1 goal: make the ball bounce, ideally in the kitchen. A ball that bounces in the kitchen can’t be volleyed hard at you, and buys you time to move up.
  1. Paddle ready. Out front, chest height, tip up.
  2. Stable beats fast. Steps, stop, balance, then hit. Never hit while running.
  3. Three ways to meet the ball: volley, short hop, step back.
  4. After every shot, choose one:
ForwardIt bounced in the kitchen
StayBounces deeper, or unsure
BackPopped up, they can volley it
  1. Ball goes high, you go low. Knees bent, paddle out, expect it at your feet.
Before players arrive

Court setup

  • Basket (~50–60 balls) at your kitchen line, center, net side. You feed from here all class.
  • Pickup bucket at the net post on the players’ side. On-deck players collect and refill between turns.
  • Zone back edge: flat cones or throw-down lines 2 ft inside the players’ baseline, both sidelines.
  • Halfway spots: 4 flat markers across the zone, about 6–7 ft behind the kitchen line, for four-across drills.
  • On-deck spot behind the players’ baseline.
  • Shadow instructor on the players’ side sideline, ready to demo.
Formats: Four across = all four players side by side, you feed in turn A→D. Pair + pair = one pair works on court while the other pair shadows the same footwork from the on-deck spot; swap after every trip.
